About Me

Building software, one detail at a time.

I'm a software developer working across web, mobile and game development, from responsive frontend interfaces to native Android and iOS apps built with Kotlin and Swift, to games built in Unity with C#. I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into something people can actually use, whatever the platform.

I've shipped 10 apps under my own Google Play developer account, including native games and utilities. My focus stays on writing dependable, well-structured code and paying attention to the small things, spacing, hierarchy, motion, performance that separate a finished product from a prototype.
